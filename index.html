Dharma Assembly Announcement

From September 3rd to 7th (Wednesday to Sunday), Thousand Buddha Temple will hold the Ullambana Dharma Assembly. Daily recitation of the "Kṣitigarbha Sutra" will begin at 9:30 AM. On the final day, there will be an Ullambana offering in the morning and a chanting session at 3:00 PM.

Everyone is welcome to participate and share the Dharma joy!
