h1:弘揚慈悲精神 祈願眾生離苦得樂

Promote the spirit of compassion and pray for all beings to be free from suffering and attain happiness

2025年8月17日，波士頓千佛寺組織了一場意義深遠的海上放生活動。當天上午，來自波士頓及周邊地區的近百位信眾齊聚麻州昆西（Quincy）渡輪碼頭，在大海之上共同踐行佛教慈悲理念，以實際行動祈願天下眾生離苦得樂，世界和平吉祥。


On August 17, 2025, the Thousand Buddha Temple in Boston organized a significant sea release event. On that morning, nearly a hundred believers from Boston and its surrounding areas gathered at the Quincy Ferry Terminal in Massachusetts. They jointly practiced the Buddhist concept of compassion on the sea, praying with their actions for all beings to be free from suffering and attain happiness, and for world peace and good fortune.


h2:登船誦經，功德回向

10點整，活動正式開始。眾人依次登船，安坐船艙。隨著渡輪緩緩駛離碼頭，寬賢法師帶領大家合掌誦經。清凈悠揚的佛號聲在甲板上回蕩，與海風和浪聲交織在一起，營造出莊嚴殊勝的氛圍。誦經過程中，法師引導信眾發願，將當日放生功德悉數回向，願諸佛菩薩慈悲護佑，令眾生早日脫離苦厄，得享安樂。



h2:Board the boat to chant sutras and dedicate the merits

At exactly 10 o 'clock, the event officially began. The people boarded the ship one by one and sat quietly in the cabins. As the ferry slowly left the pier, Master Kuanxian led everyone to join their palms and chant sutras. The pure and melodious chanting of the Buddha's name echoed on the deck, interweaving with the sea breeze and the sound of the waves, creating a solemn and extraordinary atmosphere. During the chanting of sutras, the master guided the believers to make vows, dedicating all the merits of the day's release of living beings, hoping that all Buddhas and Bodhisattvas would show mercy and protection, enabling all sentient beings to be liberated from suffering as soon as possible and enjoy peace and happiness.﻿


h2:慈悲放生，護佑海洋

船行至海灣深處，正式迎來了本次活動的核心環節——放生。寬賢法師首先為被放生的生命誦讀祈願文，慈心開示，祈求它們脫離囚禁，回歸大自然的懷抱。隨後，眾人依次將一只只龍蝦、螃蟹、小蛤蜊輕輕放入海水中。



在場信眾秉持著佛教「諸惡莫作，眾善奉行，自凈其意」的教誨，心中懷著慈悲與喜悅。每一次將生命送歸大海，都是對「萬物平等、眾生一體」的最好詮釋。許多參加活動的年輕信眾表示，這是一次極具教育意義的實踐，令他們更深刻體會到佛法中慈悲護生的精神。



h2:Board the boat to chant sutras and dedicate the merits﻿

At exactly 10 o 'clock, the event officially began. The people boarded the ship one by one and sat quietly in the cabins. As the ferry slowly left the pier, Master Kuanxian led everyone to join their palms and chant sutras. The pure and melodious chanting of the Buddha's name echoed on the deck, interweaving with the sea breeze and the sound of the waves, creating a solemn and extraordinary atmosphere. During the chanting of sutras, the master guided the believers to make vows, dedicating all the merits of the day's release of living beings, hoping that all Buddhas and Bodhisattvas would show mercy and protection, enabling all sentient beings to be liberated from suffering as soon as possible and enjoy peace and happiness.




h2:返航途中，心靈洗滌

放生活動圓滿完成後，船只緩緩返航。途中，大家或在甲板上眺望無際的海平線，或三五成群交流心得。海面波光粼粼，海鷗在空中翺翔，仿佛在為眾人作伴。許多信眾表示，在這樣清凈廣闊的環境中，內心獲得了寧靜與洗滌，仿佛煩惱與雜念也隨風而散。

﻿

On the way back, the soul was cleansed

After the release activity was successfully completed, the boat slowly returned. On the way, people either gazed at the boundless horizon from the deck or exchanged their experiences in groups of three or five. The sea surface sparkled with ripples, and seagulls soared in the air, as if keeping everyone company. Many believers have expressed that in such a pure and vast environment, their hearts have gained peace and purification, as if their troubles and distractions have also dissipated with the wind.


h2:活動圓滿，法喜充滿

約中午時分，渡輪回到Quincy碼頭。大家再次集體合影，為這次活動畫下圓滿的句號。信眾們依依惜別，紛紛表示此次活動不僅加深了對佛教慈悲精神的理解，也讓他們體會到人與自然和諧共生的重要意義。

﻿

The event was a great success and filled with joy from the Dharma

Around noon, the ferry returned to Quincy Pier. Everyone took another group photo together, bringing this event to a successful conclusion. The believers said goodbye with reluctance, expressing that this event not only deepened their understanding of the Buddhist spirit of compassion but also made them realize the significance of harmonious coexistence between humans and nature.


h2:慈悲傳承，功德無量

放生是佛教慈悲實踐的重要方式，不僅能培植善根，增長福德，更能啟發人們尊重生命、敬畏自然。本次放生活動的圓滿舉行，凝聚了千佛寺僧俗二眾的力量，也展現了波士頓佛教團體積極向善、廣結善緣的風貌。



h2:The inheritance of compassion brings boundless merit

Releasing living beings is an important way of practicing compassion in Buddhism. It not only cultivates good roots and increases merit and virtue, but also inspires people to respect life and Revere nature. The successful holding of this release event brought together the strength of both the monastic and lay communities of Qianfo Temple, and also demonstrated the positive and virtuous spirit of Buddhist groups in Boston, as well as their efforts to build good connections.

波士頓千佛寺2025年海上放生活動圓滿落幕。信眾們帶著喜悦與感動離開碼頭，心中深藏的慈悲種子，必將在日後的生活中繼續生根發芽，利益無量有情。



The 2025 sea release event of Qianfo Temple in Boston has come to a successful conclusion. The believers left the dock with joy and emotion. The seeds of compassion deeply hidden in their hearts will surely continue to take root and sprout in their future lives, benefiting countless sentient beings.
