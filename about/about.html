ABOUT US

Introduction of Massachusetts Bodhi Association

The Massachusetts Bodhi Association was founded in 1990 by Venerable Master Kuan Hsien, a disciple of Elder Master Wing Sing from Hong Kong. It is a traditional Chinese Buddhist monastery established in Quincy, Boston, Massachusetts, and is a registered non-profit Buddhist charity with the U.S. government.

The mission of the Association is to promote traditional Chinese Buddhism, benefit all beings, and serve the community. Its affiliated Thousand Buddha Temple is a place for believers to deepen their faith, study the Dharma, and practice together.

The Temple regularly holds Buddhist lectures to enhance understanding of Buddhist teachings, conducts weekly Dharma assemblies for sutra chanting, meditation, and Dharma study.

The Temple also operates a library that houses Chinese and English scriptures, Buddhist CDs and tapes for free distribution, and publishes the quarterly magazine "Buddhi Lunyin."

It has established Buddhist children’s classes, an end-of-life chanting group, and a welfare department that provides scholarships to children, aids needy families, visits and cares for the elderly, and supports global disaster relief and reconstruction efforts.

The Association is a Buddhist community organization dedicated to promoting compassion, benefiting society, and purifying people’s hearts. We welcome those who aspire to join us in spreading Buddhism, bringing peace and blessings, and contributing to the community.

Biography of Venerable Kuan Hsien

1932.9.1

Birth

Native place: Guangdong Province, China. Born on September 1, 1932.

1977

Taking Refuge

In 1977, took refuge in the Triple Gem under Elder Master Wing Sing, Abbot of Western Monastery and President of the Hong Kong Bodhi Association. Joined the Bodhi Association and attended Buddhist study classes.

1982.10

Ordination

Ordained as a monk in October 1982 at Western Monastery under Elder Master Wing Sing.

1983.12

Precepts

Received full precepts in December 1983 at Linji Monastery in Taiwan under Elder Master Bai Sheng.

1984

General Affairs

In 1984, appointed as General Affairs at the Hsu Yun Memorial Hall in Tsuen Wan, Hong Kong, under teacher’s instruction.

1995

Monastic Practice

Formally began monastic life at a monastery in Taiwan.

1985

Inheritance

In 1985, returned to Western Monastery to take charge of Dharma transmission and robes.

1988

First Visit to Boston

Visited Boston, Massachusetts in 1988, discovered the large Chinese community and many universities, but noted the lack of a proper Buddhist temple, inspiring the idea of establishing one.

1990

Establishment of Massachusetts Bodhi Association

In 1990, founded the Massachusetts Bodhi Association in Quincy with lay supporters and named it Pure Light Monastery.

1994

Planning Thousand Buddha Temple

As the number of devotees grew, planned the construction of Thousand Buddha Temple in Quincy in 1994.

2006

Bodhi Buddhist Cultural Center

In late 2006, completed the construction of the Bodhi Buddhist Cultural Center, which also includes a children’s center and library.
