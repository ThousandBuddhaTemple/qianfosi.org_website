Donation Proposal

The Origin of Fundraising for the Mass Bodhi Siksa Society Library

To whom it may concern:

As the ancient sages said, "The Dharma does not arise in isolation but manifests through circumstances. The Way is not practiced in vain but responds to karmic connections." Everything requires its own causes and conditions to be accomplished. Today, Master Kuan Hsien has established the Thousand Buddha Temple in Quincy, Boston, Massachusetts, registered as a non-profit religious organization under the name Massachusetts Budhi Siksa Society Inc. (MBSSI), to serve as a place for propagating the Dharma and benefiting all living beings. For over thirty years, it has guided countless sentient beings with karmic affinity to hear the Dharma and deeply plant their good roots. However, the Master's great vow to spread the Dharma is not yet fulfilled. He deeply feels that the mere ritual of burning incense and bowing to Buddha is insufficient to meet the needs of our time and respond to modern society. Without a profound understanding of the deep and broad teachings of Buddhism, it is impossible to widely propagate the Dharma in American society. Furthermore, Boston is a cultural and educational center in the United States, attracting a large number of intellectuals. Therefore, the Master has made a great vow to build the first Buddhist library in the history of the United States here, providing a dedicated place for Buddhist studies and religious experience in American society.

After many years of effort from various parties, the conditions for the construction of the Buddhist library are now maturing, and the construction has officially broken ground. According to the design, the library will cover a total area of approximately 10,998 square feet, with a building area of 10,407 square feet, including areas for book collection, reading, and activities. The building will be constructed with a reinforced concrete structure, following the architectural standards required by local regulations for libraries, and its design will reflect the characteristics of Chinese Buddhism. Upon completion, it can be used as a place for collecting and reading Buddhist books, holding lectures on Buddhist studies and the Dharma, and conducting activities related to Chinese culture and traditional culture for youth.

This project is expected to cost over four million US dollars, but there is still a funding gap of half the amount. Therefore, we sincerely ask all virtuous laypeople to extend their benevolent hearts, offer their strong support, and generously donate to jointly accomplish this great cause of propagating the Dharma and benefiting all living beings. This will create the conditions for the salvation of beings in this region, with immeasurable merit and benefits for generations to come! We welcome individual or joint donations. The names of those who pledge to donate $1,000 or more will be permanently displayed in the library.

For more details about the library or if you are interested in making installment donations, please call Master Kuan Hsien at (617) 773-7745 or (617) 773-1688.

The Thousand Buddha Temple is a non-profit organization, and all donations are tax-deductible.

Please make checks payable to: MBSSI

Mailing address: 53-55 Massachusetts Avenue, Quincy MA 02169

We pray that "Merit is never in vain, and blessings will surely return."

Mass Bodhi Siksa Society, Thousand Buddha Temple, Cultural and Educational Center Library:
