Library

Welcome to the Bodhi Siksa Society Library

The library is open from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

It houses a collection of various Buddhist books, audio tapes, video tapes, and DVDs in both Chinese and English.

All devotees are welcome to visit and read.

There are also Buddhist books, audio tapes, video tapes, and DVDs available for free distribution.

Those interested can visit the temple in person or write to us to request materials.
