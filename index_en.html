🏮🏮🏮 Thousand Buddha Temple Announcement 🏮🏮🏮

In honor of the Enlightenment Day of Guanyin Bodhisattva, Thousand Buddha Temple will hold a Dharma Assembly from July 31 to August 2, 2026. Each day at 9:30 AM, we will pay homage through the Great Compassion Repentance Ceremony (大悲寶懺), praying for the blessings of Guanyin Bodhisattva, world peace, prosperity and safety for all nations and peoples, and the averting of calamities. All are welcome to attend and share in the benefits of the Dharma! Meritorious offerings, such as sponsoring meals for the assembly, may be made during the Dharma Assembly — please register early!
