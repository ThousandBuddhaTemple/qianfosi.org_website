Dharma Assembly Announcement

The Thousand Buddha Temple will hold the Ullambana Festival from September 3rd to 7th (Wednesday to Sunday). Every day at 9:30, the Sutra of Kṣitigarbha Bodhisattva will be chanted. On the last day, the Ullambana offering will be made in the morning and the chanting service will be held at 3 p.m. in the afternoon.

Welcome to participate and share the joy of the Dharma!

You can also log in to ZOOM at 504-533-9965, password: 20231117.

This month's ceremony will include offerings to the deceased and other rituals. Please register early so that we can prepare accordingly!
