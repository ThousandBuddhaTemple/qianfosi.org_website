🏮🏮🏮 Thousand Buddha Temple Announcement 🏮🏮🏮

Starting June 1, Thousand Buddha Temple will observe the three-month Summer Retreat (Varsa). During the retreat, the Avatamsaka Sutra (Flower Adornment Sutra) and the Lotus Sutra will be recited daily at 9:30 AM. As the ancients said: "Without reading the Avatamsaka Sutra, one cannot know the magnificence of the Buddha's teachings." The Avatamsaka Sutra has long been honored as "the King of Sutras" and "the King of Kings." It was expounded by Shakyamuni Buddha after attaining perfect enlightenment, in the seven locations and nine assemblies among the heavenly and human realms surrounded by sages — ordinary people had no opportunity to hear it. Seven hundred years after the Buddha's passing, a great Indian master, Nagarjuna Bodhisattva, brought it from the Dragon Palace to the human realm, after which the Avatamsaka Sutra began to circulate in the world. The causes and conditions are most auspicious — all are welcome to attend!

During the recitation period, vegetarian meal offerings, longevity tablets (yansheng), and memorial tablets (chaodu) are available. Please register early if you wish to make these meritorious offerings.
