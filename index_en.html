We warmly invite you and your family to join us at Qianfo Temple for our Spring Festival Dharma Assembly. Let us come together to cultivate blessings, purify the mind, and welcome the New Year with peace and joy.

🪔 Offering lamps for blessings
🍚 Offering vegetarian meals
🙏 Offerings to the devas

Everyone is welcome—whether you are new to the temple or a longtime Dharma friend. Let us rejoice together in the Dharma and share in its blessings.
